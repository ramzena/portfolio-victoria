About Me

The storyteller behind the screen

I am a bestselling romance author with over 20 years of professional writing experience and 170+ published novels across dark romance, contemporary romance, fantasy, and psychological drama.

Published under multiple pen names — Uliana Soboleva, Melania Soboleva, and others — my books have reached over 500,000 readers on platforms like LitRes, MyBook, Bookmate, and Amazon.

In 2024, I expanded into AI-powered video production, independently creating cinematic trailers, animated series, and vertical short drama content. I handle the entire pipeline: writing, visual generation, voice acting, sound design, editing, and post-production.

My unique value: I am that rare combination of a master storyteller who understands cliffhanger-driven episodic pacing AND the technical ability to produce AI video content at scale. Most AI creators lack narrative depth. Most writers lack production skills. I deliver both.
